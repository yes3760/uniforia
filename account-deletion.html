Account and Data Deletion

How to delete your Uniforia account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and personal data in the Uniforia app, published by Yunus Emre Sevindik.

In the app

  • Open Uniforia and sign in.
  • Tap your profile photo, then Settings.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately. You are signed out when the deletion is complete.

If you cannot access the app

Send an e-mail with the subject "Account deletion request" to neyapiyyi@gmail.com from the university e-mail address registered in the app. Once we confirm the e-mail comes from the registered address, the request is completed within 30 days at the latest and you are notified.

Data that is deleted

  • Your profile: full name, username, profile photo, bio, department, year and interests
  • All images you uploaded
  • Your posts, comments, polls, votes and likes
  • Your friendships, friend requests and blocks
  • Your event registrations, QR tickets and check-in records
  • Events you created for yourself
  • Your club memberships and membership applications
  • Reports you made, suspension records on your account and your consent records

Data that is kept, and for how long

  • Messages you sent: stay in the recipient's conversation, with the sender information removed.
  • Events you created for a club: belong to the club and stay there, with the link to you removed.
  • Backups: copies in backups are deleted within 30 days at the latest.
  • Security logs (IP address and time): kept for at most 2 years as required by Law No. 5651 and related legislation.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can delete your posts and comments one by one, remove your profile photo and change your profile information in Settings. Deleted posts and comments are removed from view immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days.

See the Privacy Notice for details.
