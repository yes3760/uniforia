Child Safety Standards

Uniforia's standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation

Uniforia, published by Yunus Emre Sevindik, is a campus social network for university students and staff. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

What is prohibited

  • Any text, image or message that depicts, praises or encourages the sexual abuse of children
  • Content showing people under 18 in a sexual context
  • Attempting to build a sexual relationship with a child (grooming), sexual extortion (sextortion) and trafficking
  • Linking to, sharing, requesting or selling such content

These rules are part of the Community Rules in the Terms of Use and apply to posts, comments, profiles, events and messages.

How to report

  • In the app, use Report on a post, comment or profile (reason: "Inappropriate content"). The person you report is not told your name.
  • Use Block to stop a person from contacting you.
  • By e-mail: neyapiyyi@gmail.com (subject: "Child safety"). These reports are reviewed with priority.

What we do

  • We review reported content with priority and remove it immediately if it violates these standards.
  • We permanently close the account involved.
  • We report child sexual abuse material to the competent authorities in Türkiye (the General Directorate of Security and İhbar Web: ihbarweb.org.tr) and, where required, to the relevant international organisations.
  • We securely keep the records needed for investigations for the period required by law.

Legal compliance

We comply with the laws on child safety, including the Turkish Criminal Code, Law No. 5651 and related legislation, and with Google Play policies.

Point of contact

For questions and reports about child safety: Yunus Emre Sevindik, neyapiyyi@gmail.com
