Uniforia Notice on the Processing of Personal Data

Version: 2026-10-28 · Effective date: 28 October 2026

This English translation is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Turkish text, the Turkish text prevails.

This notice explains how your personal data is processed when you use the Uniforia mobile app (the "App") and the services offered through it (the "Service"), as required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform.

1. Data controller

Your personal data is processed by Yunus Emre Sevindik ("we") as the data controller.

  • Address: [doldurulacak: ADRES]
  • E-mail for personal data requests: neyapiyyi@gmail.com
  • E-mail for support: neyapiyyi@gmail.com

2. Personal data we process

We only process the data needed to provide the Service:

  • Identity: your full name and username.
  • Contact: your university e-mail address.
  • Education and professional information: your university; if you are a student, your department and year; your account type (student or staff) and your verification status, which is set from your e-mail domain.
  • Images: your profile photo, the images you add to your posts and the cover images of events you create.
  • Profile information: your bio, the interests you choose and your app language.
  • Your posts: your posts, comments, polls, poll votes and likes.
  • Social connections: friend requests you send and receive, your friendships and the people you block.
  • Messages: the content of messages you exchange with friends, and when they were sent and read.
  • Events and clubs: your event registrations and waitlist position, your personal QR ticket, when you checked in to an event and the staff member who recorded it; your club memberships, club role and membership applications; the events you create.
  • Legal and compliance records: the versions of the Terms of Use you accepted and of this Privacy Notice you acknowledged, with the time; reports you make and reports about you (reason and note); moderation decisions and suspensions on your account (duration and reason); support and request correspondence with us.
  • Security: session information, records of sign-in codes sent and verified, and system logs containing IP addresses and timestamps.
  • Waitlist: if your university is not supported yet, the e-mail address and domain you leave so we can let you know.

We do not access your location or contacts. Only the photos you pick are uploaded from your device. The camera is used only when you open it, to scan QR tickets at event check-in; no footage is stored. Adding an event to your calendar happens in your device's calendar app; your calendar is not sent to us.

Kept only on your device: your theme and language settings, your unfinished event draft and your session key are stored only on your device. Check-ins scanned without a connection wait on the staff member's device until a connection is available, and are then sent to us.

Special categories of personal data: We do not ask for, and do not separately process, data on race, ethnic origin, political opinion, philosophical belief, religion, sect or other beliefs, appearance and dress, membership of associations, foundations or trade unions, health, sex life, criminal convictions and security measures, or biometric and genetic data (KVKK Article 6). Please do not include such information in your profile, posts or messages. If you do, it is stored within the technical operation of the Service only to be shown to the people you choose.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process your personal data for the following purposes, relying on the legal bases in Article 5(2) of the KVKK.

a) Membership and authentication

Sending a sign-in code to your e-mail address, determining your university and account type from your e-mail domain, creating your account and keeping you signed in.

  • Data: identity, contact, education and professional information, security.
  • Legal basis: processing is necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract with you (Art. 5(2)(c)).

b) Providing the Service

Creating and showing your profile; posts, comments, polls, likes and messaging; friendships and blocking; club membership and club management; creating events, registration, waitlists, QR tickets and check-in; suggesting and filtering events by your interests; storing and showing images.

  • Data: identity, contact, education and professional information, images, profile information, posts, social connections, messages, events and clubs.
  • Legal basis: Art. 5(2)(c).

c) Security, abuse prevention and moderation

Reviewing reports, hiding content that breaks the Community Rules, temporarily suspending accounts, applying your blocks, preventing fake accounts and unauthorised access, and limiting how often sign-in codes can be sent.

  • Data: identity, posts, social connections, legal and compliance records, security.
  • Legal basis: Art. 5(2)(c), and processing necessary for our legitimate interests, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Art. 5(2)(f)). Our legitimate interest is keeping users and the Service safe.

d) Legal obligations

Our obligations as a hosting provider under Law No. 5651 on the Regulation of Internet Publications; responding to lawful requests from authorities and courts; answering requests under the KVKK; keeping consent records.

  • Data: identity, contact, posts, messages, legal and compliance records, security.
  • Legal basis: processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation (Art. 5(2)(ç)).

e) Legal disputes

Establishing, exercising or defending a right, for example using records as evidence in a dispute.

  • Data: limited to the dispute, identity, contact, posts, messages, legal and compliance records, security.
  • Legal basis: Art. 5(2)(e).

f) Support and notices

Answering support and data requests; telling you about important changes to the Service (for example, updates to this notice or the Terms of Use).

  • Data: identity, contact and the information you share in your request.
  • Legal basis: Art. 5(2)(c) and Art. 5(2)(ç).

g) Waitlist

Letting you know when your university becomes supported.

  • Data: the e-mail address and domain you leave on the waitlist.
  • Legal basis: at your request, directly related to a contract to be concluded (Art. 5(2)(c)).

We do not process personal data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell your data. The App contains no advertising and no third-party tracking (analytics) tools. We do not send you commercial electronic messages without your consent; sign-in codes and notices about the Service are not commercial messages.

4. How we collect data

Your personal data is collected by automated means, electronically:

  • from information you enter in the App (sign-up, profile, posts, messages, events and clubs);
  • from records created while you use the App (sessions, timestamps, IP addresses);
  • from other users' actions (messages and friend requests sent to you, the staff member who records your check-in, a club admin changing your role, reports about you);
  • from your correspondence when you e-mail us.

5. Who can see your information in the Service

  • Your profile (full name, username, profile photo, bio, department, year, interests, club memberships): verified users at your university.
  • Your profile photo and event cover images: so that they can be displayed, they are stored at addresses that are hard to guess but can be opened by anyone who has the link.
  • Your posts and comments: verified users at your university; club-only posts are visible to club members only. You and the people you block, or who block you, do not see each other's posts and comments.
  • Events: "public" events are visible to verified users at all universities, "university" events to verified users at your university, and "members only" events to club members.
  • Your registration and check-in: the person who organises the event and, for club events, club admins with check-in permission.
  • Your messages: only the two people in the conversation.
  • Your reports: the people we appoint as moderators. The person you report is not told your name.
  • Suspensions and moderation decisions: you and the moderators who made the decision.

We access your data only as far as needed to run the Service, keep it secure, fix errors and meet our legal obligations.

6. Transfers of personal data

a) Our service providers

  • Supabase, Inc. (USA): database, authentication and file storage infrastructure. Data is stored on servers in Germany (Frankfurt, European Union).
  • Resend, Inc. (USA): sending sign-in code e-mails (only your e-mail address and the content of the e-mail).

These providers process data on our behalf and on our instructions, as data processors. The legal bases for these transfers are Art. 5(2)(c) and Art. 5(2)(f).

b) Transfers abroad

Our service providers are established abroad and may process data on servers abroad. These transfers are made under Article 9 of the KVKK by signing the standard contracts announced by the Personal Data Protection Board and notifying them to the Personal Data Protection Authority (Art. 9(4)).

c) Authorities

To public authorities and courts that are legally entitled to request data, on request and to the extent required by law (Art. 5(2)(ç)); and to our lawyers and the courts when needed to establish, exercise or defend a right (Art. 5(2)(e)).

d) No other transfers

Other than the above, we do not transfer personal data to your university or to any other third party. Uniforia is not an official app of any university.

External links: Directions on an event page open Google Maps, and external registration links open the website named by the organiser. When you tap such a link, that third party processes data under its own privacy notice and policies and is responsible for that processing.

7. Retention periods

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was processed for:

  • While your account is open: your profile information, posts, social connections, messages, event and club information, and consent and moderation records.
  • When you delete your account: the files you uploaded, your profile, posts, comments, likes, votes, friendships, blocks, event registrations, club memberships, the reports you made, your suspension records and your consent records are deleted immediately. Events you created for yourself are deleted; events you created for a club belong to the club and stay there, with the link to you removed. Messages you sent stay in the recipient's conversation, with the sender information removed.
  • Posts and comments you delete: removed from view immediately; kept for 30 days for possible reports and legal requests, then permanently deleted.
  • Content hidden by moderation: kept until your account is deleted, for appeals and legal requests.
  • Images you replace or remove: deleted immediately. Uploaded images that are never used in a post are deleted automatically after 24 hours, and unused event covers after 30 days.
  • Waitlist: until your university is supported and you are notified, for at most 2 years.
  • Security logs: for the period required by Law No. 5651 and related legislation, at most 2 years.
  • Support and request correspondence: 2 years after your request is closed.

When these periods end, the data is deleted or anonymised in line with the Regulation on the Deletion, Destruction or Anonymisation of Personal Data. Copies in backups are deleted when the backup retention period ends, within 30 days at the latest.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information about it if it is,
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred,
  • ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected,
  • ask for the data to be deleted or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7 of the KVKK,
  • ask for corrections, deletions and destructions to be notified to the third parties the data was transferred to,
  • object to a result against you that arises from analysis of the data exclusively by automated systems,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because the data was processed unlawfully.

You can correct your profile information in the App yourself, and delete your account yourself under Settings → Delete account.

9. How to make a request

Under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, you can send us requests about your rights in Turkish and in writing:

  • by a signed letter to [doldurulacak: ADRES],
  • by e-mail to neyapiyyi@gmail.com from the e-mail address registered in the App.

Your request must include your full name; your signature if it is a written request; your Turkish ID number if you are a Turkish citizen, or your nationality and passport or ID number if you are not; your residential or business address for notifications; your e-mail address for notifications, if any; your phone or fax number; and the subject of your request.

We will resolve your request as soon as possible, and within 30 days at the latest, free of charge. If the request requires additional cost, a fee set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged. If your request is rejected, you find our answer insufficient or we do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request (Art. 14).

10. Users under 18

The Service is meant for university students and staff. If you are under 18, you may only use the Service with the permission of your parent or guardian.

11. Changes

We update this notice when our processing changes. The current version is always available in the App under Settings → Legal. When the notice changes, we show you the current version when you open the App.
